# Research frontier

## Active gate: G007

Given an arbitrary primitive rational middle Hodge class \(\zeta\), start with
the global monodromy tube detector supplied by B011. Construct an algebraic
two-parameter degeneration that concentrates its global cancellation at one
higher discriminant stratum, producing a type-\((0,0)\) Saito relation
\(\beta\) with
\(\langle\zeta,\gamma_\beta\rangle\ne0\). B010 then propagates to G006 and
B007 to the terminal conjecture.

Immediate bricks:

1. Model a filling disk for a Schnell loop and its intersections with the
   discriminant using vanishing thimbles and relative homology.
2. Identify geometric conditions under which several meridians can specialize
   to one nodal or isolated-singularity member.
3. Prove or refute preservation of the tube's primitive class under that
   specialization using Saito's exact sequence.
4. Keep the type-\((0,0)\), rational, and unipotent conditions explicit;
   nodal fibers satisfy them automatically, arbitrary singularities do not.
5. Keep the semiregularity gate G004 as an independent secondary route.

If concentration fails, record whether the obstruction is nonexistence of the
required higher discriminant incidence, loss of the tube class under
specialization, loss of Hodge type, or annihilation by the class-specific
pairing. Do not promote a favorable family.
