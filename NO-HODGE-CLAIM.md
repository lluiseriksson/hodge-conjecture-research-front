# No claim of a proof or disproof

This repository does not prove or disprove the standard rational Hodge
Conjecture. It records definitions, reductions, conditional gates, source
audits, experiments, and failed routes.

A future terminal claim must handle every smooth projective algebraic variety
over \(\mathbf C\), every codimension, and every rational Hodge class; construct
an actual rational algebraic cycle (or a valid counterexample); survive all
field, coefficient, specialization, and equivalence audits; reproduce from a
cold clone; and receive independent expert scrutiny. Numerical evidence, an
algebraic Hodge locus, a special family, or a hypothesis package is not enough.
