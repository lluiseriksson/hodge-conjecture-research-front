# G129 — Construct the augmented nodal generator package

Let

\[
 P_m=(R_+J)_m,\qquad
 Q_m=J_m/P_m,\qquad
 D_m=K_m/P_m.
\]

G129 takes the minimal new-double branch of B199:

\[
 \dim D_m=1,\qquad
 \dim V_m=2n,\qquad
 0\longrightarrow D_m\longrightarrow Q_m
 \longrightarrow V_m\longrightarrow0. \tag{1}
\]

The line \(D_m\) must be generated by a nonzero section \(F\) whose divisor
has an isolated ODP at every point of \(Z\). A complementary
\(2n\)-dimensional generator space must map isomorphically to \(V_m\), and
every node-gradient map from \(V_m\) must be an isomorphism. The pulled-back
inverse Hessians of \(F\) must satisfy B193's conformal holonomy.

At degree \(m-1\), require \(V_{m-1}=0\); B197 then supplies all earlier
extinctions. In the same full degree-\(m\) incidence, close G126's finite
Kuranishi ladder and retain the no-coloop value relation, rational
type-\((0,0)\) detector, and nonzero pairing with the specified class.

G129 is a sufficient subgate of G128, not a claim that every possible
solution has a new central generator. The inherited-double branch of B199
remains open and requires a separate lower-Hessian construction.
