# B178 — Differential rigidity of the escape ideal

Use B158's notation. Choose a rank-\(R\) branch basis \(B\), let

\[
 F_B=V(\tau_b:b\in B),
\]

and write

\[
 \epsilon_{B,i}=\tau_i|_{F_B},
 \qquad
 K_B=(\epsilon_{B,i}:i\notin B)
 \subset\mathcal O_{F_B,0}. \tag{1}
\]

Every escape component vanishes at the origin, so

\[
 K_B\subseteq\mathfrak m_{F_B}. \tag{2}
\]

By B158,

\[
 H_\tau=0\Longleftrightarrow K_B=0. \tag{3}
\]

## Differential-ideal lemma

Let \(O=\mathbf C\{u_1,\ldots,u_q\}\) and let
\(K\subseteq\mathfrak m_O\) be an ideal. The following are equivalent:

1. \(K=0\).
2. \(\partial_{u_j}K\subseteq K\) for every \(j\).
3. \(\theta K\subseteq K\) for every
   \(\theta\in\operatorname{Der}_{\mathbf C}(O)\).
4. \(K\) is stable under one \(O\)-basis of
   \(\operatorname{Der}_{\mathbf C}(O)\).

Only 2 implies 1 needs proof. Suppose \(K\ne0\), and choose
\(g\in K\setminus\{0\}\) of minimal vanishing order \(m\). Condition
(2) on the ideal makes \(m\ge1\). Some partial derivative of the nonzero
degree-\(m\) initial homogeneous form is nonzero, so for some \(j\)

\[
 \operatorname{ord}_0(\partial_{u_j}g)=m-1.
\]

Stability places this derivative in \(K\), contradicting minimality.
Thus \(K=0\). Conditions 2--4 are equivalent because the coordinate
derivations and any local frame generate the tangent module over \(O\).
For \(q=0\), (2) already gives \(K\subseteq0\).

Applying the lemma to (1) and combining it with (3) gives

\[
 \boxed{
 H_\tau=0
 \Longleftrightarrow
 K_B=0
 \Longleftrightarrow
 \operatorname{Der}_{\mathbf C}(\mathcal O_{F_B,0})K_B
 \subseteq K_B.
 } \tag{4}
\]

## Relation to logarithmic fields

Choose coordinates \((f_B,u)\) on the full parameter germ. Tangent lifts
of a frame \(\partial_{u_j}\) preserve the basis ideal \((f_B)\). Modulo
that ideal, their failure to preserve the full ideal \(I_\tau\) is exactly

\[
 \partial_{u_j}\epsilon_{B,i}\pmod {K_B}.
\]

Thus (4) is the restriction-to-\(F_B\) form of B176's logarithmic-orbit
criterion. It is an equivalent construction target, not an automatic
connection on \(K_B\) and not a Hodge-theoretic conclusion.
