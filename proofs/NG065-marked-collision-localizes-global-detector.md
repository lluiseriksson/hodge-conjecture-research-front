# NG065 — Marked collision geometry does not localize the detector class

**Status:** NO-GO

B089 gives a fixed reference fiber and a locally braid-invariant boundary
loop around a chosen independent-node target. B058 gives an unrelated global
loop-fixed pair whose tube pairs nontrivially with the prescribed Hodge
class.

No formal operation identifies these pairs. The local boundary loop may
have no fixed class with nonzero ambient image, and its entire local relation
space may map into the orthogonal complement of the prescribed Hodge class.
B008 also shows why replacing the target by a generic smooth-discriminant
point cannot help.

B090/NG066 prove that the positive total local boundary is not a viable
re-entry. G054 must instead retain the nonlocal B058 word and prove that its
special value in the B089 disk has nonzero prescribed pairing.
