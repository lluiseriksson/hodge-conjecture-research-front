# NG228 — Quartic equality does not survive

- **Label:** NO-GO
- **Route:** realize G190 with \(A=O_Q(4)\) at rank \(7d+5\).
- **Easy locus:** a good perfect matching gives a cubic unit; its square
  times the complete quadratic system supplies all \(d+1\) residual
  jets.
- **Hard locus:** if four supports lie on one line through \(u\), B272
  constructs an independent zero-value transverse octic.
- **Normal directions:** degree-seven value interpolation supplies all
  \(d-2\) normal residual coordinates.
- **Rank consequence:** residual rank is at least \(d\), so total rank
  is at least \(7d+6\).
- **Conclusion:** quartic equality is impossible for every even
  \(d\ge22\).
- **Boundary consequence:** together with NG227, G190 is NO-GO; B266
  then makes G191 NO-GO, and G192 is the next active piecewise gate.
- **Detector guard:** no ODP package, Kuranishi vanishing, rational
  detector, specified pairing, cycle, proof, or disproof of HC is
  produced.
