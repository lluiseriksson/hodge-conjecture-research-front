# G171 — Move one rank beyond the nonstandard boundary

B247 closes G170 and the adjacent odd layer. The next balanced
signature is

\[
 m=2,\qquad s=6d+8,\qquad \delta_1=3d+4, \tag{1}
\]

with

\[
 N=8d+10,\qquad h_Z(1)=4d+5=N/2,\qquad
 s-2\delta_1=0. \tag{2}
\]

Every polarization must be re-audited at this new rank. On valid
quadrics of even dimension \(d\ge10\), B246 still excludes the standard
polarization because \(5d-3>4d+5\); the borderline dimension \(d=8\)
does not permit that simplification.

G171 remains only a sufficient specialization of G148. No marked
scheme, ODP package, rational detector, specified pairing, algebraic
cycle, proof, or disproof of HC is currently constructed.
