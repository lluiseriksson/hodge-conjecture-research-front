# G134 — Construct a primitive quadratic-profile birth

Let \(W_\bullet\) and \(E_\bullet\) be B204's graded profile and value
spaces. G134 asks for the exact degree-\(m\) birth

\[
 \frac{W_m}{\sum_{a=1}^mE_aW_{m-a}}
 =\mathbf C[q_{t,Q}], \tag{1}
\]

where \(q_{t,Q}\) is nondegenerate at every marked point. In addition,

\[
 H^0(I_Z^3H^m)\subset P_m \tag{2}
\]

must eliminate the triple-hidden term of B203.

Equations (1) and (2) imply G133's one-dimensional new double-generator
line. Lift \(q_{t,Q}\) to a central section \(F\) with isolated ODPs and
G130's congruence, while retaining adjacent first-jet birth, the no-coloop
value relation, rational type-\((0,0)\), and the nonzero specified pairing.

G134 is the smallest current graded construction obligation inside G130.
It does not construct its profile line or imply G131's cubic filters.
