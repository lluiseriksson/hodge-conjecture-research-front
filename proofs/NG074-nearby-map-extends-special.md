# NG074 — The nearby ambient map does not automatically extend specially

**Status:** NO-GO

B098 fixes the generic quotient map and its value on the B057 chain. B010
separately fixes the special Saito ambient map. B083 supplies only the arrow
from a special stalk to nearby cycles; it does not prove that these two
ambient maps agree across a topology-changing boundary. B081 also requires
the special class to land in the correct perverse grade and full-support
summand.

Thus the nearby equality $q_P(t_\psi)=c$ does not prove
$\Phi_H(r_H(\beta))=q_P(u\beta)$. The re-entry condition is G062: prove that
comparison as a rational Hodge-module identity on the actual collision.
