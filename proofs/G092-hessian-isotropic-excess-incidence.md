# G092 — Hessian-isotropic integrable excess incidence

## Exact theorem sought

For every

\[
0\ne\zeta\in
H^{2n}_{\mathrm{prim}}(X,\mathbf Q(n))\cap H^{0,0},
\]

construct \(L=H^m\), a nodal member \([s]\in|L|\), ordered nodes
\(\Delta\), and a rational vanishing-cycle relation \(\beta\) such that:

1. the node-value matroid is uniform \(U_{R,N}\), \(1\le R<N\);
2. the analytic ideal generated by the smoothing functions
   \(\tau_1,\ldots,\tau_N\) of B146 is radical of height \(R\) and is
   locally generated by \(R\) functions with independent differentials;
3. the conditional gradient image is totally isotropic for every B146
   Hessian relation form, hence has corank at least \(n(R+1)\);
4. the adjoint defect is positive and
   \(\langle\zeta,\gamma_\beta\rangle\ne0\).

The second item is invariant under changing local frames, which multiplies
the individual branch equations by analytic units. A choice of defining
functions for which \(\tau\) has constant rank \(R\) is one sufficient
certificate. In either formulation, \(\tau^{-1}(0)\) is a reduced smooth
codimension-\(R\) germ.
B145 then gives G090, B144 gives the clean arrangement, and B054/B134-B135
identify the rational local functional. Thus G092 is sufficient for G091
and hence for rational HC through B007.

## Attempt 1 — A generic value-rank degeneracy locus

A determinantal construction can force \(\operatorname{rank}E=R<N\), but
it does not force the smoothing equations to become redundant. If

\[
D:\ker E\to\bigoplus_iT_{p_i}^*X\otimes L|_{p_i}
\]

is surjective, B146 gives a nonzero second-order obstruction for every
nonzero value relation. The incidence is then nonreduced or singular in a
tangent direction, rather than a smooth excess component. NG117 records
this failed route.

## Smallest remaining construction

The first geometric obligation is to produce, without selecting an
algebraic representative of \(\zeta\), a rank-deficient node scheme for
which the **gradient** interpolation map loses at least \(n(R+1)\)
dimensions in precisely the common isotropic subspace dictated by the node
Hessians. One must then integrate this special first-order package so that
the full smoothing ideal is reduced and generated by exactly \(R\)
independent equations.

Known smooth excess examples do not supply this universally. S032's
maximal defective nodal hypersurfaces contain a linear space, and S061's
smooth non-expected equianalytic examples are special orbit-type
unisingular families. Neither gives a carrier-free construction from an
arbitrary pair \((X,\zeta)\), nor a nonzero specified Saito pairing.
