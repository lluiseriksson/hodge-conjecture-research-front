# NG132 — Pointwise conormal absorption misses normal-cone escape

- **Route:** quotient only the ambient characteristic covectors lying over
  \(F_B\), or check their containment in \(N^*_{F_B}P\) at the central
  point or along the reduced intersection.
- **Valid input:** for B168's smooth branch
  \(\ell_N(x)+y^m=0\), its ambient conormal over \(F_B\) is generated by
  \(d\ell_N\) and is pointwise contained in \(N^*_{F_B}P\).
- **Invalid inference:** the microlocal inverse-image transform has only
  zero covectors.
- **Precise obstruction:** the scheme-theoretic pullback is \(y^m=0\).
  Its reduced divisor \(y=0\) carries a rank-one ODP specialization group,
  so \(i^\#\) contains \(T^*_{\{y=0\}}F_B\). The normal cone remembers the
  higher-order contact discarded by the pointwise quotient.
- **Jet guard:** choosing \(m\) above any prescribed order preserves the
  proposed finite tangent/jet data.
- **Re-entry condition:** prove the full germwise inclusion in G106 using
  \(i^\#\), not pointwise conormal restriction, and retain the specified
  detector pairing separately.
