# NG239 — Cubic rank \(7d+6\) does not survive

- **Label:** NO-GO
- **Route:** retain the final cubic branch of G200.
- **Incidence obstruction:** absence of two disjoint secants avoiding an
  eighth point would place six of seven supports on one line through
  that point.
- **Rank obstruction:** at least five of B260's six supports would then
  be collinear, forcing their six-double rank to be at most
  \(6d+3<6d+6\).
- **Separator:** two pair hyperplanes, three singleton hyperplanes, and
  one common hyperplane give a sextic double at all seven supports but
  not double at the eighth.
- **Boundary consequence:** G200 is NO-GO and G203 is active.

  rational detector, specified pairing, cycle, proof, or disproof of HC
