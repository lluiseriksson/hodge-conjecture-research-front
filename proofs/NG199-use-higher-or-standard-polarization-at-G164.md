# NG199 — Higher and standard polarizations do not survive G164

- **Route:** retain a powered polarization \(O_Q(k\ge3)\), or use the
  standard polarization uniformly in dimensions at least six.
- **Valid premise:** G164 adds a third point-span dimension beyond the
  three tangent osculators.
- **Invalid inference:** that dimension absorbs every later marked
  tangent.
- **Higher-power obstruction:** the six-factor construction gives a value
  or transverse first-jet separator at the seventh point.
- **Standard obstruction:** quotient ranks close every \(d\ge8\). In
  \(d=6\), residual equality cannot contain a third point, while the other
  branch has contact locus only one plane conic plus one point.
- **Detector guard:** no ODP package, rational detector, specified pairing,
  cycle, proof, or disproof of HC is produced.
- **Conclusion:** the only G164 quadric-survival branches are
  \(O_Q(2)\) for \(d\ge6\), and \(O_Q(1)\) or \(O_Q(2)\) for \(d=4\).
  G164 and HC remain open.
- **Re-entry condition:** attack G165's square-polarized incidence and the
  exceptional standard \(Q^4\) branch.
