# G191 — Conditional square/standard/cubic boundary

Under B265's now-retracted cubic/quartic floor, the next balanced
signature would have been

\[
 h_Z(1)=M(d),\quad
 \delta_1=M(d)-d-1,\quad
 s_5(d)=2(M(d)-d-1),\quad N=2M(d), \tag{1}
\]

where

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
 d & M(d) & s_5(d) & \text{polarizations not yet excluded}\\ \hline
 14,16,18,20 & 6d+6 & 10d+10 & A=O_Q(2)\\
 22 & 159 & 272 & A=O_Q(1)\\
 d\ge24\text{ even} & 7d+6 & 12d+10 & A=O_Q(3),O_Q(4).
\end{array} \tag{2}
\]

Conditional G191 would classify square equality in the four low
dimensions, standard equality at \(d=22\), and cubic/quartic equality
in even dimensions at least 24, then retain every G144 relation, ODP,
Kuranishi, rational-type, and nonzero specified-pairing clause. It is
not the active gate.

B267 retracts the cubic/quartic floor used to reach this boundary.
Accordingly G191 is now only CONDITIONAL and inactive. B266 still
excludes the standard rank \(8d-17\), but planar cubic/quartic rank
\(7d+5\) keeps G190 open. No detector, pairing, cycle, proof, or
disproof of HC is produced.
