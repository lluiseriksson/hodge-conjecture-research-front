# NG227 — Cubic planar equality does not survive the marked scheme

- **Label:** NO-GO
- **Route:** realize G190 at the cubic equality rank \(7d+5\).
- **Jet reduction:** equality gives residual plane sextic rank one;
  B270 transfers this to zero anticanonical differential.
- **Geometric classification:** B271's six-point weak del Pezzo has only
  \(A_1/A_2\) effective-root components. Zero differential forces an
  \(A_2\) intersection, hence complementary collinear triples through
  \(u\).
- **Extension obstruction:** B269 separates every eighth double from
  every such \(3+3\) span.
- **Cardinality contradiction:** G190 requires \(N=2(7d+5)>7\) marked
  supports with every tangent osculator absorbed.
- **Conclusion:** cubic equality is impossible on the quadric test
  input for every even \(d\ge22\).
- **Remaining branch:** quartic equality for even \(d\ge22\), together
  with the low-dimensional square cases, remains open.
- **Detector guard:** no ODP package, Kuranishi vanishing, rational
  detector, specified pairing, cycle, proof, or disproof of HC is
  produced.
