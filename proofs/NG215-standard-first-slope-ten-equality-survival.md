# NG215 — Standard survival at first slope-ten equality

- **Label:** NO-GO
- **Route:** attain \(h_Z(1)=6d-7\) with \(A=O_Q(1)\).
- **Valid premise:** B253's final branch has exactly \(d-4\) dimensions
  left, equal to its first rank-one escape bound.
- **Invalid inference:** equality of those dimensions permits the filled
  span to retain a large contact locus.
- **Exact-rank obstruction:** contraction of
  \(\operatorname{Sym}^2J\), \(\dim J=d-3\), has rank \(d-3\) unless
  the last point \(x\) lies in \(J\). Equality forces \(x\in J\).
- **Residual-annihilator obstruction:** after filling the span,
  \(\operatorname{Sym}^2(J\cap x^\perp)\) remains in the annihilator,
  with \(\dim(J\cap x^\perp)=d-4\).
- **Point-rank obstruction:** every marked point is confined to the
  resulting \(\mathbf P^5\), of quadratic point rank at most twenty-one,
  below \(6d-7\).
- **Common-floor consequence:** \(h_Z(1)\ge6d-6\) and
  \(s\ge10d-14\) for every polarization.
- **Universal-quantifier guard:** every even \(Q^d\), \(d\ge14\), is a
  valid input. No special-family success is promoted upward.
- **Detector guard:** no ODP package, rational detector, specified
  pairing, cycle, proof, or disproof of HC is produced.
- **Conclusion:** G183 and \(s=10d-15\) are closed. G148 and HC remain
  open.
- **Re-entry condition:** G184 begins at
  \(s=10d-14,\delta_1=5d-7,N=12d-12,h_Z(1)=6d-6\), with only
  \(A=O_Q(1)\) surviving at equality.
