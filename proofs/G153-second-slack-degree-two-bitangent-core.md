# G153 — Construct the second-slack degree-two bitangent core

B228/NG187 close every first-slack specialization. B222 shows that the
next numerical layer is already viable at \(m=2\), \(s=2\). B229 makes
its exact finite geometry

\[
 |Z|=4n+4,\qquad \dim E_1=2n+2,\qquad
 E_1=E_1^{\perp_\lambda}. \tag{1}
\]

G153 asks for this self-associated code inside the chosen
\(H=A^2\)-image of \(X\), with every marked tangent space a hyperplane
in its span. Every pair must simultaneously fail interpolation on
\(2p\sqcup2q\); equivalently at the necessary ambient level, every
secant in the A-embedding is tangent to \(X\) at both endpoints.

The same marked scheme must carry the central degree-two ODP generator,
the complete adjacent profile and finite Kuranishi closure, a
full-support rational type-\((0,0)\) relation, and nonzero Saito pairing
with the specified \(\zeta\). None of these clauses follows from the
self-associated or bitangent configuration alone.
