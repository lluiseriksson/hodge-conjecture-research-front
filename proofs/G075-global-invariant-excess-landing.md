# G075 — Land the selected excess in global invariant full support

**Status:** EXPLORATORY  
**Parent gate:** G074

Let (M) be the full global (S_3)-equivariant nearby-cycle coefficient
object for the actual collision, and let (e_t) be the selected excess
constructed from the B058 detector. Decompose its canonical relevant
perverse grade by strict support and then by (S_3)-isotypic type. Prove

\[
 \operatorname{pr}_{\mathrm{fs}}!left(e_{S_3}e_t\right)\ne0.
\]

The calculation must exhibit the actual global thimble-extension coordinate
of (e_t). B113/NG089 exclude a vector living only in the local (A_2)
standard representation. B077/NG054 exclude inferring class landing from the
mere existence of the full-support object, and B081 requires the canonical
perverse grade rather than a chosen total splitting.

After this landing is proved, G074 must still establish ordinary liftability,
survival through both B022 kernels, and nonzero prescribed pairing.

## No double counting

G075 is the selected-excess formulation of the first nonvanishing clause in
G042. It does not constitute an additional reduction or independent progress
toward the general conjecture. Its purpose is to make G074's first admissible
geometric calculation explicit after the local (A_2) route fails.
