# G163 — Construct two dimensions beyond three doubles

B239 excludes G162 and its adjacent odd layer. The first remaining
degree-two signature is

\[
 m=2,\qquad s=4d+8,\qquad \delta_1=2d+4. \tag{1}
\]

It has

\[
 N=6d+10,\qquad h_Z(1)=3d+5=N/2,\qquad
 s-2\delta_1=0. \tag{2}
\]

The relation transport is an isomorphism and the degree-one code is
diagonally self-dual.

G163 asks for this code on every primitive target together with the full
ODP, adjacent-profile, holonomy, finite-Kuranishi, rational
type-\((0,0)\), and specified-pairing package. At exponent six, three
doubles plus two reduced points now fill the available span; the next
audit must determine whether a third reduced point or its tangent can be
absorbed in every incidence pattern.

G163 remains only a sufficient specialization of G148. No marked scheme,
detector, pairing, algebraic cycle, proof, or disproof of HC is currently
constructed.
