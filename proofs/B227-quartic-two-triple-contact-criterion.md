# B227 — Quartic two-triple defect forces a common asymptotic chord

Put \(p=[1:0:\cdots:0]\), \(q=[0:1:0:\cdots:0]\) in
\(\mathbf P^r\). At \(p\), use \(x_i=X_i/X_0\); at \(q\), use
\(y_0=X_0/X_1\) and \(y_i=X_i/X_1\), \(i\ge2\).

Every order-at-most-two monomial at \(p\), except \(x_1^2\), is the
restriction of a quartic monomial vanishing to order at least three at
\(q\). The symmetric statement holds at \(q\). The two exceptions are
both represented by the single monomial \(X_0^2X_1^2\). Therefore the
ambient image is the hyperplane

\[
 [x_1^2]_p=[y_0^2]_q. \tag{1}
\]

Its cokernel has dimension one. Let \(\lambda\) be the difference of
the two coefficient functionals in (1).

The intrinsic jet target on \(X\) is a quotient

\[
 \pi:J^2_{\mathbf P^r,p}\oplus J^2_{\mathbf P^r,q}
 \longrightarrow J^2_{X,p}\oplus J^2_{X,q}. \tag{2}
\]

The ambient quartics map to \(\pi(\ker\lambda)\). This image fails to
fill the intrinsic target exactly when \(\lambda\) annihilates
\(\ker\pi\), so that it descends to a nonzero intrinsic functional.
The condition is local at both endpoints.

At \(p\), let \(v_p\) be the direction of the secant line. The
coefficient functional \([x_1^2]_p\) annihilates the degree-at-most-two
part of the ideal of \(X\) exactly when

\[
 v_p\in T_pX,\qquad \mathrm{II}_p(v_p,v_p)=0. \tag{3}
\]

Indeed, multiplying a linear local equation by a linear function first
forces \(v_p\in T_pX\); evaluating the quadratic initial forms then
gives the second condition. Equivalently, every local equation of \(X\)
restricts to the secant line with order at least three at \(p\). The
same argument applies at \(q\).

When (3) holds, the descended local functional is nonzero: \(x_1\)
restricts to a nonzero tangent parameter in direction \(v_p\), so
\(x_1^2\) has a nonzero intrinsic quadratic jet.

Thus ambient quartics fail on the intrinsic two-triple target precisely
for a secant with contact order at least three at both endpoints. Since
ambient quartics form a subspace of \(H^0(X,A^4)\), failure of the full
restriction map implies this contact condition. If the embedding is
4-normal, every \(A^4\)-section is ambient, so the converse follows and
the cokernel is the one-dimensional quotient generated by \(\lambda\).

B227 is a jet criterion only. It constructs no marked clique, ODP
family, rational detector, pairing, or cycle.
