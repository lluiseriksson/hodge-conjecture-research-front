# G140 — Realize the class-directed fat-point signature

For arbitrary \((X,\zeta)\), construct \(Z\) and \(m\ge2\) with

\[
 \begin{array}{c|cc}
 &h_{2Z}-h_Z&h_{3Z}-h_{2Z}\\ \hline
 m-1&0&0\\
 m&2n&1
 \end{array} \tag{1}
\]

and satisfy B211's node floor whenever the degree-\(m-1\) order-two jet
fiber has full rank. The one-dimensional final profile must be generated by
the nondegenerate central ODP section, not merely have the correct rank.

Retain \(H^0(I_Z^3H^m)\subset P_m\), the quadratic congruence, conformal
holonomy, full tangent system, no-coloop value relation, rational
type-\((0,0)\), and the specified nonzero pairing.

B211 and B210 make (1) the finite Hilbert-function core of G139. The rank
table alone does not construct the distinguished profile, detector, pure
cubic closure, later rungs, or a cycle.
