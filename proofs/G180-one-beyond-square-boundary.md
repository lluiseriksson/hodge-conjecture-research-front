# G180 — One rank beyond the square boundary

B253 excludes the standard polarization at \(h_Z(1)=5d+4\), B254
excludes the square polarization, and B249 excludes every
\(O_Q(k)\), \(k\ge3\). Hence G180 and the adjacent odd layer
\(s=8d+7\) are NO-GO on valid even quadrics \(Q^d\), \(d\ge14\).

This is only a necessary special-input obstruction. It constructs no
marked scheme, ODP package, rational detector, specified pairing,
algebraic cycle, proof, or disproof of HC.
