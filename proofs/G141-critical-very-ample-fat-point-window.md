# G141 — Construct inside the critical very-ample window

For arbitrary \((X,\zeta)\), construct \(Z,m,r,R\) with

\[
 \begin{array}{c|ccc}
 &h_Z&h_{2Z}&h_{3Z}\\ \hline
 m-1&r&r&r\\
 m&R&R+2n&R+2n+1
 \end{array}, \tag{1}
\]

subject to

\[
 N\ge m+2,\qquad m\le r\le R,\qquad m+1\le R\le N-1. \tag{2}
\]

The degree-\(m\) value matroid must have no coloop; by B212 every one of
its nonzero relations then has support at least \(m+2\). Retain B211's
node floor whenever its pointwise jet hypothesis holds.

The unique final quadratic-profile line must still be generated by the
nondegenerate central ODP member. All G140/G130 holonomy, congruence,
full-system, no-coloop rational detector, and specified-pairing clauses
remain mandatory. Equations (1)--(2) do not construct any of them.

B213 couples the value spaces across complementary degrees using one
full-support degree-\(m\) relation. It strengthens (2) to
\(N\ge2n+1+\max\{m,2n+1\}\) and imposes
\(h_Z(a)+h_Z(m-a)\le N\) for every \(1\le a<m\). G142 is the refined
gate carrying these actual transport maps; NG175 closes the smaller
numerical window left open by B212.
