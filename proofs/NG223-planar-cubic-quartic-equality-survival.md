# NG223 — Distinct complementary units do not separate planar jets

- **Label:** NO-GO
- **Attempted route:** use two distinct planar secants to produce two
  different annihilator graph planes and combined rank \(d\).
- **Valid premise:** the complementary products have jets
  \((1,\Lambda-\lambda_e)\) that vary with \(e\).
- **Invalid inference:** these are the unit generators of the full
  variable-edge product images.
- **Missing factor:** the variable edge contributes
  \((1,\lambda_e)\), and

  \[
  (1,\lambda_e)(1,\Lambda-\lambda_e)=(1,\Lambda).
  \]

- **Exact obstruction:** every planar edge image equals
  \(\langle(1,\Lambda)\rangle\oplus\operatorname{Ann}(U)\), of rank
  \(d-1\).
- **Consequence:** the claimed cubic/quartic floor \(7d+6\) is
  retracted; G190 remains open on the planar locus.
- **Downstream guard:** G191 and G192 are only conditional boundaries,
  not active gates or general progress.
- **Detector guard:** no ODP package, rational detector, specified
  pairing, cycle, proof, or disproof of HC is produced.
- **Re-entry condition:** use sections outside the span generated by
  varying one pair-line factor at a time, or find a different geometric
  obstruction to planar equality.
