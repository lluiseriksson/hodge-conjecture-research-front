# G178 — Five ranks beyond slope-eight equality

B253 excludes the standard polarization at \(h_Z(1)=5d+2\), while
B249 excludes the square and every higher polarization. Hence G178 and
the adjacent odd layer \(s=8d+3\) are NO-GO on valid even quadrics
\(Q^d\), \(d\ge10\).

This is only a necessary special-input obstruction. It constructs no
marked scheme, ODP package, rational detector, specified pairing,
algebraic cycle, proof, or disproof of HC.
