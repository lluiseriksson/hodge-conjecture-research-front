# G185 — The third standard slope-ten boundary

B258 raises the common quadric floor to

\[
 m=2,\qquad s=10d-12,\qquad \delta_1=5d-6,\qquad
 N=12d-10,\qquad h_Z(1)=6d-5=N/2. \tag{1}
\]

On every even quadric of dimension at least fourteen, every
nonstandard polarization remains strictly above equality. The standard
branch corresponds to B253's parameter \(q=d-4\).

G185 is the next falsifiable gate: extend the nested rank-one escape
audit through the budgets \(d-4,d-3,d-2\), while retaining every G144
relation, ODP, Kuranishi, rational-type, and nonzero specified-pairing
clause. Rank survival alone would not construct an algebraic cycle or
prove or disprove HC.
