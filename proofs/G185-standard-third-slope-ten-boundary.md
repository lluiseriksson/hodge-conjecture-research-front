# G185 — The third standard slope-ten boundary

B258 raises the common quadric floor to

\[
 m=2,\qquad s=10d-12,\qquad \delta_1=5d-6,\qquad
 N=12d-10,\qquad h_Z(1)=6d-5=N/2. \tag{1}
\]

On every even quadric of dimension at least fourteen, every
nonstandard polarization remains strictly above equality. The standard
branch corresponds to B253's parameter \(q=d-4\).

B259 makes the nested escape parametric. For
\(q=d-4+r\), \(0\le r\le d-8\), every mixed branch leaves at most
\(d-2+r\) dimensions. The first escape consumes at least \(d-4\);
the residual \(r+2\le d-6\) is strictly below the second escape
\(d-5\). The residual smaller-quadric branch is also strictly below
B246. Hence the standard floor is \(7d-12\).

Combining with the nonstandard floor \(6d+6\) gives the piecewise
common floor

\[
 F(d)=\min\{7d-12,6d+6\}. \tag{2}
\]

Thus G185 and every lower common layer are NO-GO. The next gate is G186
at \(h_Z(1)=F(d)\). No ODP package, rational detector, specified
pairing, algebraic cycle, proof, or disproof of HC is produced.
