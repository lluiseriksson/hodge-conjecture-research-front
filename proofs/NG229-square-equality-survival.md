# NG229 — Square equality does not survive

- **Label:** NO-GO
- **Route:** realize G192 with \(A=O_Q(2)\) at rank \(6d+6\) in one of
  the dimensions \(14,16,18,20\).
- **Confinement obstruction:** if all marked points lie in the standard
  linear span \(W\) of the six independent supports, their quartic point
  span lies in \(\operatorname{Sym}^4W\), but an absorbed tangent contains
  \(v^3u\) for \(u\in v^\perp\setminus W\).

  separated from the six doubles by B254's hyperplane square, and its
  entire \(d+1\)-dimensional double neighborhood is independent.

  \(7d+7\), not \(6d+6\).
