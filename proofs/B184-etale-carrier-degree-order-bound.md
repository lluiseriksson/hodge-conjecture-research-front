# B184 — Étale-carrier degree bound for escape order

Let \(V\subset\mathbf A^M\) be irreducible of dimension \(q\ge1\), smooth
at \(p\), and let its projective closure have degree

\[
 \delta=\deg \overline V.
\]

Let \(N\in\mathbf C[x_1,\ldots,x_M]\) have degree at most \(e\), and
assume that \(N|_V\) is nonzero and vanishes at \(p\).

## Degree bounds local order

Put \(m=\operatorname{ord}_p(N|_V)\). In regular parameters at \(p\), the
degree-\(m\) initial form of \(N|_V\) is a nonzero homogeneous polynomial
on \(T_pV\). Choose a tangent direction on which this initial form is
nonzero.

Intersect \(V\) with \(q-1\) sufficiently general affine hyperplanes
through \(p\), chosen so that their tangent intersection is that direction.
The component \(C\) through \(p\) is a curve smooth at \(p\), is not
contained in \(N=0\), and satisfies

\[
 \operatorname{ord}_p(N|_C)=m,
 \qquad
 \deg\overline C\le\delta. \tag{1}
\]

For \(q=1\), take \(C=V\). Projective Bézout applied to
\(\overline C\) and the homogenization of \(N\) gives

\[
 m
 \le I_p(\overline C,V(N))
 \le e\deg\overline C
 \le e\delta. \tag{2}
\]

Thus every nonzero numerator has order at most \(e\delta\). The carrier
degree is essential: on \(V_s=(y-x^s)\), the degree-one function \(y\)
has order \(s=\deg V_s\) at the origin.

## Étale descent of the conormal map

Let

\[
 \pi:(V,p)\longrightarrow(F_B,0)
\]

be a pointed étale morphism, let \(O=\mathcal O_{F_B,0}\),
\(O'=\mathcal O_{V,p}\), and let \(K\subset O\) be the escape ideal. The
local étale map \(O\to O'\) is faithfully flat and

\[
 K'=KO',\qquad
 K'/K'^2\simeq(K/K^2)\otimes_OO',\qquad
 \Omega^1_{O'}\simeq\Omega^1_O\otimes_OO'. \tag{3}
\]

Under these identifications, the pulled-back conormal map is

\[
 \beta_{K'}=\beta_K\otimes_OO'. \tag{4}
\]

Faithful flatness therefore gives

\[
 \beta_K=0\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\beta_{K'}=0. \tag{5}
\]

## Finite carrier certificate

Suppose that after B183 unit clearing, \(K'\) is generated by restrictions
of polynomials \(N_i\) of degree at most \(e\). If \(K'\ne0\), choose a
nonzero generator of minimum order \(m\). Equation (2) gives
\(m\le\delta e\). A partial derivative of its initial form has order
\(m-1\), while every element of \(K'\) has order at least \(m\). Hence
\(\beta_{K'}\ne0\) and is visible by order at most

\[
 \delta e-1. \tag{6}
\]

Consequently,

\[
 j^{\delta e-1}\beta_{K'}=0
 \Longrightarrow \beta_{K'}=0
 \Longrightarrow \beta_K=0
 \Longrightarrow H_\tau=0. \tag{7}
\]

This replaces B180's simple-polynomial requirement by two explicit
geometric numbers: the degree of an algebraic étale carrier and the degree
of its cleared numerator representatives.

## Scope guard

B184 does not construct the carrier from an arbitrary class-directed full
linear system, bound its degree, bound the numerator representatives, or
prove the jets in (7) vanish. It supplies only the finite implication once
those data are audited.
