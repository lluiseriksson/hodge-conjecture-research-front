# NG096 — Full plane-local invariance is unnecessary

**Status:** NO-GO

- **Route:** require the selected nearby vector to be fixed by every
  generator of the local fundamental group of the smooth locus in a
  two-dimensional ball around the collision.
- **Valid input:** local invariant cycles requires invariance under the local
  group of the base to which it is applied.
- **Invalid inference:** that base must remain the whole plane germ.
- **Precise obstruction:** B120 applies the theorem after proper base change
  to one marked curve. Its punctured disk has cyclic fundamental group, and
  its special-fiber group is canonically the same fiber cohomology that
  appears as \(H^{-1}(i_p^*K_B)\) after the normal-slice shift.

Full invariance is genuinely stronger. Let

\[
 0\longrightarrow J=\mathbf Qj
 \longrightarrow A=\mathbf Qe\oplus\mathbf Qj
 \longrightarrow V=\mathbf Q\bar e
 \longrightarrow0
\]

and let two commuting generators act by

\[
 M_1=I,
 \qquad
 M_2(e)=e+j,
 \qquad
 M_2(j)=j.
\]

The quotient \(V\) is constant. The class \(\bar e\) has the invariant lift
\(e\) along the cyclic subgroup generated by \(M_1\), but no lift
\(e+aj\) is fixed by \(M_2\), since

\[
 (M_2-I)(e+aj)=j.
\]

Thus one-curve liftability does not imply full plane-local invariance, and
the latter cannot be retained as a necessary condition.

- **Re-entry condition:** construct G082's class on one explicit original
  collision disk and kill its cyclic B085 obstruction. Simultaneous
  multi-direction invariance is optional unless a later argument explicitly
  uses compatible nearby identifications for several approach curves.
